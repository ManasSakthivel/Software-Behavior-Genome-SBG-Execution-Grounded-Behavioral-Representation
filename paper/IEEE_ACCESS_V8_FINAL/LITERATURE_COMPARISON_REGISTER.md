# Literature Comparison Register (SBG V8, IEEE Access)

All 70 entries in `references.bib` were re-verified on **2026-11-19**. Verification codes:

- **CR**: DOI resolved through the Crossref REST API (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`). Title, authors, venue, year and pages were checked against the bib entry.
- **AX**: arXiv export API (`export.arxiv.org/api/query`). Title, authors, first-version date and abstract were checked. "No journal_ref" means arXiv records no venue.
- **VP**: venue or publisher page fetched (JMLR, PMLR, NeurIPS proceedings, ICLR proceedings, USENIX, slsa.dev), or a full-text PDF read.
- **FT**: full text, or substantial parts of it, read in this or an earlier sprint. Earlier reads are listed in `research/v8_final_design/SOURCE_ACCESS_REGISTER.csv`.

"Contribution" is taken from the abstract, or from the paper where FT is marked. Where only metadata and the abstract were seen, the description stays at abstract level. Do not cite these papers for anything more specific.

Columns: **Supports** = the claim in our paper that the reference may support. **Must NOT be cited for** = misuses to avoid.
