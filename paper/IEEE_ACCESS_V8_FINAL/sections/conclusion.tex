\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

An empty difference set is evidence that no divergence was observed on the inputs that were run, within the channels that were watched. This paper treated the corresponding verdict as a selective prediction and asked how often it is wrong and whether its reliability can be estimated from the evidence behind it. We defined the false-assurance risk of \NDOfull{} verdicts relative to a declared observation boundary and adjudication budget; built a change-conditioned evidence profile with integrity, provenance and environment signals; specified an Evidence Response Signature of eight properties tested by nine controlled perturbations; and placed evidence collection behind an authenticated, sealed and adversarially tested pipeline.

On a 312-pair development corpus the results separate what works from what remains open. As a label-free conformance test, the Evidence Response Signature detected a real defect in the evidence profile, separated the \sbg{} trust gate from its predecessor, and exposed the fitted model's reliance on a single feature. Matched-coverage evaluation showed that the predecessor's sufficiency gate abstained heavily without making its remaining verdicts more reliable. As a predictor, change-conditioned calibration with or without \ers{} features was not shown to reduce false-assurance risk relative to coverage-only calibration ($+1.6$ points, interval $-6.6$ to $+16.3$, sign unstable across seeds), and no calibrated model achieved risk control under a conservative threshold rule. The study was powered to detect only differences of about 16 points. Result-channel and post-hoc tampering by parties without the anchor key force abstention rather than a false verdict; in-process harness forgery and evidence substitution are detected but not prevented; and has at least one open evaluation-awareness channel.

The present evaluation therefore shows that the false-assurance estimand can be operationalised and that the evidence-response protocol can detect estimator defects, while the incremental predictive value of the richer evidence over coverage-derived information remains inconclusive. Settling that question requires an independently adjudicated external corpus of several hundred to over a thousand issued verdicts, for which the protocol, the prediction lock and the evaluation code are in place.
