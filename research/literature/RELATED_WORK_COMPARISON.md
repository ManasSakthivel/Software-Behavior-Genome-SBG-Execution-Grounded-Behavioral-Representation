# Related Work Comparison (capability table)

Labels: **Supported** = the capability is stated in the source at the access level shown. **Partially supported** = a related mechanism is stated but not the full capability as defined. **Not supported** = a FULL TEXT source (read in the P0 pass) shows the work does not provide it, or states it as a limit. **Not evaluated** = the source is silent on it at the access level shown, and I did not look further (silence is NOT evidence of absence). **Unknown** = access level too low to judge. Access in brackets: FT full text, AB abstract, MD metadata.

Capability definitions: (A) compares old and new versions by executing both; (B) produces a re-checkable witness artefact; (C) records how much of the change was exercised (sufficiency); (D) has an explicit inconclusive/insufficient outcome grounded in observation evidence; (E) handles nondeterminism/flakiness in the comparison; (F) evaluates on real changes.
