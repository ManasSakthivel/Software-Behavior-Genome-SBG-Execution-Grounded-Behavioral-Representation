# SBG V7 Risk Register

Companion to `SBG_V7_IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md` (gates G0-G9, kill checkpoints K1-K5 are defined there) and `_DESIGN_SPINE.md`.
Scales: Likelihood (L) and Impact (I) are L/M/H, judged for the Python-first programme as planned. "Owner role" is a role, not a person. Status of every risk at design time: open.

Roles: PI = principal investigator/programme lead; STAT = statistician; ENG-CAP = capture/infra engineer; ENG-AN = analysis/verdict engineer; ENG-BM = benchmark/eval engineer; SEC = security reviewer (can be a part-time external); ADJ = adjudication lead; CUST = holdout custodian (not on the build team).

## Strongest arguments against the whole V7 programme

1. It may be differential testing in new clothes. For deterministic units, output divergence on a witness input is already a sound bug signal; the V6 evidence supports this (delta_out 0.995 on v10, 0.976 on QuixBugs evaluable). Internal-state divergence beyond that is unproven, and internal differences are exactly what refactorings produce, so "more signal" can be "more noise".
2. The binding constraint is observation, not representation. V6 coverage on real bugs was 28.5% and BugsInPy AUROC was chance. If better exploration is the real lever, the correct programme is an input-generation/activation study with a trivial detector, not a trace-graph system. V7 spends most effort (P5, P6, views, invariants) on the part that the failure analysis did not implicate.
3. Ground truth for the interesting claim (masked divergence, "insufficient" calibration) is the scarcest, noisiest thing in the field. If only a few dozen real masked cases exist, the central claim is untestable with confidence, and synthetic suites are circular by construction (the V6 lesson).
4. Novelty may be thin. Regression test generation, differential/shadow symbolic execution, regression verification, Daikon-style invariant diffing and spectrum-based localisation already cover most of the pipeline; the integration plus calibrated abstention may be engineering rather than science.
5. Cost versus benefit. 32+ calendar weeks and 50-70 engineer-weeks, much of it benchmark labour, for a result that could be a null. A V6 post-mortem already shows a similar pattern: conventional features matched or beat the novel representation.
6. Three-valued verdicts shift rather than remove the burden: users get many INSUFFICIENT outputs on real code (likely > 50%), which limits practical value.
7. Executing untrusted code at scale carries real security cost and ongoing operational burden.

Responses (what would change this assessment): K4 shows a measured gain at equal budget; K3 shows activation uplift; K1/K5 show a real masked stratum of adequate size. If none occurs, the programme should reduce to the smaller sufficiency-aware differential tool or stop.

## Conditions under which V7 should be stopped (or cut back)

Stop entirely (publish the negative result and the observation-layer artefacts) if ANY of:
- S1: K1 fails (< 60 runnable independent real-defect pairs) AND controlled suites cannot support a claim distinct from V6 (no way to test the question).
- S2: K2/K3 both fail: capture-determinism < 50% after controls AND exploration-derived activation < 50% with uplift < +10 pp over replay, so the system cannot observe real code.
- S3: K4 fails twice (end P3 and end P4): no recall, false-assurance, or time-to-detection advantage over output differential at equal budget, AND the sufficiency calibration (R03) is not informative. Then the residual product is "differential testing with honest coverage reporting", which is a tooling note, not a programme.
- S4: Any unresolved sandbox escape after one remediation cycle (R11), or inability to run untrusted code under policy.
- S5: Two consecutive gate slips of > 4 weeks each with no scope reduction accepted by the PI (R29), or loss of the benchmark/adjudication capacity before K5.
- S6: Leakage found in the sealed holdout that cannot be repaired (R27/R28): do not unseal; rebuild first.

Cut back (reduce to core path, drop P5/P6/P9) if: K4 passes marginally (gain < +3 pp real, only on synthetic masked suite); P5 ablation shows no retained view; budget or calendar exceeds the 90th percentile estimate (48 weeks to P8).
