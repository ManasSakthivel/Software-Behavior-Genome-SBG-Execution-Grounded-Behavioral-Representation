**Status: SBG V8 MAC EXECUTION PARTIALLY COMPLETE — BLOCKERS DOCUMENTED.** All Mac-feasible work was executed; items below that depend on Linux, independent data or human adjudication, plus listed open defects, remain.

1. **Both successful attacks fixed?** Yes. A08 (stdout hiding) is detected by a parent-side independent capture; P04 (edit unverified fields and re-chain) is detected by a schema-checked capture file bound to the log and a MAC anchor. P04 counts as detected only where the verifier holds the key and the attacker does not.
2. **Original 15-attack suite?** Rerun with the original 15 unchanged; the two former successes are now detected (before/after JSON in `results/v8_mac_security/`). Added attacks A16, A17, P09 are detected; P08 (a writer who holds the anchor key and re-anchors everything) still succeeds by design and needs an out-of-band pinned head.
3. **Unverified guarantees:** a program that replaces `sys.stdout` itself or forges records after reading the per-run nonce (same address space, M-4); binary stdout recorded in decoded form; incomplete pipe reads inferred; key custody beyond controller memory/env; thread safety of the supervisor; Linux isolation.
4. **Evidence collection outside the evaluated process?** The authoritative stdout/stderr capture and anchor are held by the parent controller; the harness still runs inside the evaluated process, so in-process manipulation by the program remains possible (pinned by a test). Process separation is not an OS sandbox.
5. **Full regression:** `.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/v7 tests/v6 tests/v8 -q` -> **541 passed, 1 skipped, 2 xfailed, 7 warnings in 1,464 s** (baseline 419 passed, 1 skipped; +122 V8 tests; the 2 xfails document known profile defects and are expected failures, not removed tests).

For the fitted model, the passes for P2, P3, P4, P6, P7, P9 mean "no wrong-direction response" because it reads only `mu_min` (deltas exactly 0). Open defects: count features (`n_same`, `n_rep`, `n_eff`, `n_eff_prov`) credit irrelevant inputs and unflagged duplicates; an unfitted full-profile model fails post-hoc P2 on 109 of 227 applicable records. The repository defines nine perturbations; the master prompt lists eight properties.
7. **Real-execution tests:** P2 (applicable 7 of 30 pairs, 7 passed), P5 (30 of 30), P6 (applicable 1 of 30; no independent discriminating input derivable for the other 29). Selection rule pre-declared; a 10-pair supplement was added afterwards and is recorded as post hoc; two collector versions were mixed in the primary run.
8. **Primary pool policy defensible?** Strict `v8.1` (both blockers hard) is primary by declared semantics: V7-B reports INSUFFICIENT and an NDO on never-executed changed code is not defensible; `v8.0` stays reproducible. Consequence: the eligible pool falls 85 -> 61 (equal to V7-B's NDO set), activation is constant among eligible pairs, so the coverage hypothesis is untestable on this data. `budget_exhausted_s0` never occurs in development data (code review only).
9. **Matched-coverage free of held-out threshold selection?** The existing within-fold rank matching uses no held-out labels but its cut depends on the held-out score distribution (transductive): a diagnostic, not deployable. A separate frozen-threshold evaluation (thresholds learned on calibration projects only; label-poisoning test) was added. Its result: no risk control at alpha 0.10/0.15/0.20; the conservative rule issues nothing and the optimistic rule gives held-out risk 0.24-0.50 above target.
10. **CEC improvement over coverage-only calibration?** No demonstrated improvement. Under v8.1 all models collapse to the V7-B gate at target coverage; ERS log-loss gain +0.005 to +0.057, paired cluster CI includes 0 in 5 of 6 runs, placebo p about 0.07 or lower in 5 of 6; multiplicity-only matches full CEC. Honest out-of-fold base-rate Brier is 0.231 (v8.0), not the earlier in-sample 0.203 (corrected).
11. **Statistical limitations:** development data only, generator labels, one dominant project, small eligible n (61-85), unstable slopes (CIs of width 1-10, mostly a leave-project-out artefact of near-constant predictors), about 30 unadjusted contrasts, fold seeds are replicates not independent samples.
