## 2. Contradiction matrix

Severity key. **Blocking**: two or more documents give incompatible rules for a decision that every later phase depends on, or a downstream decision gate cannot be evaluated; P0 cannot freeze without resolving it. **Major**: a conflict or omission that would change a gate outcome, a metric, or an implementation, but can be resolved locally. **Moderate**: a numeric or naming conflict with limited blast radius. **Minor**: wording, arithmetic, or traceability nit.
