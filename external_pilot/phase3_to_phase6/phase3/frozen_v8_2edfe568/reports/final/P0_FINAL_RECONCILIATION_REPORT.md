## 0. Explicit statement of claims

No equivalence claim and no correctness claim is made by this report, by the frozen spec, or by any verdict V7-B can emit. The verdict set is `DIVERGENT_OBSERVABLE`, `INSUFFICIENT(reason)`, `NO_DIVERGENCE_OBSERVED(S1|S2|S3)`. `NO_DIVERGENCE_OBSERVED` reports what was looked at, never that versions are equal (SPEC Theorem 2: S3 is compatible with divergent versions). `EQUIVALENT`, `SAME`, `PASS`, `VERIFIED`, "implicit pass" are prohibited outcomes; `BOUNDED_EQUIVALENCE_PROVEN` exists only as a separately named formal claim with a stated domain. Nothing here has been run; no V7 code exists; every threshold is an UNVALIDATED default.
