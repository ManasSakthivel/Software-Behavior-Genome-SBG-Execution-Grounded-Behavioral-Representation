# 06 Experimental protocol (to be frozen and time-stamped before any Phase 3 label is seen)

## Research questions and hypotheses
RQ1 Does phi(E) predict false assurance better than structural coverage alone?
 H1a: held-out log loss and Brier score of the tier-1 model on phi are lower than those of the coverage-only model (a, b only); H1a-null: no difference. Test: paired project-cluster bootstrap on per-pair loss difference.
RQ2 Does CEC reduce false-assurance risk at matched decision coverage?
 **Primary hypothesis H2:** at matched NDO coverage c* the selective risk R of CEC is lower than that of coverage-only calibration (baseline 7) by at least 5 percentage points absolute, with the one-sided 97.5% project-cluster bootstrap lower bound of the difference above 0. Matched coverage grid pre-declared: c* in {0.20, 0.30, 0.40} plus c* = the coverage of the V7-B fixed gate on the same fold; the primary c* is the V7-B-gate coverage; the others are secondary. No operating point is chosen after seeing results.
 Null: difference < 5pp or CI includes 0.
RQ3 Do controlled perturbations expose weaknesses not captured by static coverage and repeated execution?
 H3: adding response features rho(E) (full CEC, baseline 8) lowers held-out log loss and selective risk at matched coverage versus the same model without them (baseline 9). Also P1, P2, P4 hold on 100% of a fixture set (deterministic property tests).
RQ4 Does the method retain calibration on project-held-out evaluation?
 H4: calibration slope on the final holdout has a project-clustered 95% CI containing 1 and intercept CI containing 0, and the Brier score is below that of the base-rate predictor. Reported with intervals; "retain" is not claimed if the CI is wide enough to be uninformative (width > 0.6 on slope).
## Baselines (all executed on the same pairs and the same folds)
1 B1 plain differential testing; 2 B1+repetition; 3 V7-B fixed gate (S1/S2 as is); 4 coverage-only gate (changed-line activation + branch floor, no other rule); 5 coverage-gated B1 (issue NDO iff coverage floor satisfied, otherwise abstain); 6 matched-abstention baseline (abstain on a pair-agnostic random subset or by random score to match coverage; averaged over 1,000 draws); 7 coverage-only calibration (calibrated risk from a, b); 8 full CEC; 9 CEC without response features. Additional: a label-free redundancy baseline (mu_min alone) to test whether T_del merely re-expresses multiplicity. No baseline is dropped for weakening results.
## Metrics
Primary: selective false-assurance risk R among issued NDO at matched coverage. Secondary: coverage at risk <= alpha_pre (0.10, 0.15); detection recall (DIV on Y=1 pairs); abstention rate; Brier; log loss; calibration intercept and slope; reliability diagrams; ECE with sensitivity; response-property pass rates; runtime overhead (CPU seconds per pair, multiple of V7-B). Abstention is never reported as an improvement without matched coverage.
## Statistics
Unit of resampling: project. 10,000 cluster-bootstrap resamples (percentile and BCa both reported); paired differences; one-sided for the primary, two-sided otherwise. Multiple comparisons: Holm over the primary family {H2 vs baseline 7, H2 vs baseline 3, H2 vs baseline 6}; H1a, H3, H4 are separate pre-declared families. Practical threshold 5pp. Sensitivity: unresolved pairs as false assurances; without hard negatives; project-macro R; leave-one-project-out; alpha_pre alternatives; alternative canonicalisation. Missing data: runs that fail or time out count as INSUFFICIENT for every system; no pair silently dropped; unresolved labels counted and reported. Power (arithmetic in 07, assumed discordance 10-20% of matched issued pairs, design effect 1.45): a 5pp matched-coverage difference needs about 450-910 matched issued verdicts and an 8pp difference about 175-350. A 600-pair study yields about 150-240 issued verdicts, so its minimum detectable effect is roughly 8-10pp: **H2 at the 5pp threshold is underpowered at the K5-scale dataset.** Pre-declared handling: the 5pp threshold stays as the practical-significance criterion, the study reports the realised interval and MDE, a non-significant result is NOT read as no effect, and the grouped-CV estimate on the whole non-holdout pool (not the holdout alone) is the primary inference. To power 5pp the dataset would need roughly 1,100-2,300 pairs at issuance 0.4-0.2.
## Pre-registration
Freeze before labelling: predictors, monotone directions, model class, penalty grid, alpha_pre, c* grid, baselines, exclusion rules, resampling scheme, holdout projects (selected by random seed written in the registration), analysis scripts hashed. Publish the registration (hash in the repository, time-stamped by a third party such as OSF) before the holdout is scored. Development data (321 pairs) may be used for debugging only; they are design-exposed.
## What can falsify the central claim
H2 fails or H3 fails on the holdout and grouped CV; or calibration slope CI excludes 1 materially; or property tests P1/P2/P4 fail. Any of these produce a negative or weak result and are reported as such (10 contains the fallback framings).
