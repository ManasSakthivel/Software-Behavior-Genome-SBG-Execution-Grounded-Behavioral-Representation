## 1. What SBG V7-B is, at HEAD (source: `docs/architecture/V7B_IMPLEMENTATION.md`, `reports/phase2/FINAL_SPECIFICATION_DECISIONS.md`)

- Scope: Python, two versions of a single file, entry = top-level function, user-supplied JSON-value inputs; no input generation, no explorer, no S3.
- Witness: `CONFIRMED_DIFF` on a channel (outcome, exception, side_effect, api), requiring verified, untampered primary, repeat, replay and alternate runs (SD-10). Replay `r_replay` fresh runs per side; alternate control runs `k_alt` shift RNG/time/urandom (not `datetime.now`, not hash seed).
- Three verdicts: `DIVERGENT_OBSERVABLE` > `INSUFFICIENT` (primary reason plus secondaries from an 18-code enum) > `NO_DIVERGENCE_OBSERVED` (S1 or S2). No equivalence value exists in the enum.
- Sufficiency: S1/S2 floors on changed-line activation (by deterministic, clean SAME inputs) and, for S2, 80% branch-direction coverage on changed lines; thresholds are UNVALIDATED defaults. `r_empty` (no changed unit) is recorded; capping it at S1 (UD-2 option B) is recommended, not applied.
- Abstention reasons (examples, not exhaustive): `changed_region_not_activated`, `unmodelled_change` (A-U1), `unobservable_outcome` (A-L1 lossy/degenerate SAME), `nondeterministic`, `no_inputs`, `resource_limit`, `unsafe_program`, `sandbox_failure`, `tool_error`. Conservative blocking: any quarantined input and any `NO_RUN` blocks NDO (UD-4 option A).
- Determinism: child env allowlist, `PYTHONHASHSEED=0` actually reaching the child (since MB-07), `python -s -P -B`. Hash-order sensitivity untested (Decision A); `datetime.now` not virtualised, so false witnesses are possible (Decision B).
- Evidence: hash-chained `evidence.jsonl` (ProgramVersion with source, EvidencePackage with config/inputs/regions/rows, Observation, Run, Witness, SufficiencyRecord, Verdict) and `python -m sbgv7 verify` (re-derives per-input outcomes from stored runs and re-runs `decide.py`; nothing is re-executed; observations taken as recorded). Plaintext unkeyed chain: detects accidental corruption only, not tamper-evident, not tamper-proof; no trust anchor. Isolation: macOS `sandbox-exec` only, `policy_below_minimum=true`; no validated OS isolation, no Linux path.

## 2. Empirical status of SBG (the only evidence that exists)

All of it is development-grade, exploratory, on non-pristine data (321 development pairs: 31 QuixBugs, 85 QuixBugs-neg, 150 synthetic, 55 BugsInPy units; developer-owned or previously exposed). No independent or confirmatory result exists; K1 has not been evaluated on an independent real-project benchmark (UD-5 open). Specifically:
- E1 (`reports/phase2/E1_K2_DETERMINISM_REMEASUREMENT.md`): hypothesis "QUARANTINED rate does not rise" SUPPORTED weakly: 1 vs 2 pairs of 312 driven (1 vs 3 inputs of 3,044), underpowered, clustered by project, confounded with other engine changes (old vs current engine, not a pure hash-seed effect). Not a determinism confirmation.
- Same run: 63 of 312 verdicts changed; DIVERGENT_OBSERVABLE unchanged at 84; NDO/S2 fell 91 -> 55; INSUFFICIENT rose 120 -> 157 (37 NDO/S2 became `unmodelled_change`). Whether this reduces false assurance was inspected for 3 pairs only; no ground-truth analysis.
- Verify: all 414 evidence directories report `verified: true`. This shows self-consistency of the logs, nothing more.
- Earlier internal red-team findings (cited in the Comparison file, not re-read here): S2 reachable from two inputs and no measured discrimination on synthetic labels; replay/alt machinery differed from a repeat-three-times baseline on 2 of 311 pairs; engine gaps dominated INSUFFICIENT. Pre-MB-07 K1/K2 numbers are declared not comparable.
- No comparison against any cited tool or baseline has been run. No result in this file or in the literature set supports superiority over any work below.

## 4. Reclassification of C1 to C5

Scale: Not novel / Partially novel / Novel in combination / Unsupported. This is a single-reviewer, limited-access assessment; "not found" is not "does not exist", and no "first" claim is made for any item.

| Claim | Earlier (pass 2 file) | Phase 2 classification | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 witness representation (replayable record for difference and no-difference outcomes with observation context) | Partially novel (narrowed) | **Novel in combination, weakly**; the executable-witness component is **Not novel** | Executable, replayable difference artefacts are routine (Shadow, Mokav, Testora, PatchGuru, Evans & Savoia; verification witnesses). What no accessed text defines is one hash-chained schema carrying the observation context and no-difference outcomes together with a verify command. That is an absence claim bounded by 20 full texts (12 tool-mediated), and Jin 2010, Rutledge & Orso 2022, Shadow TOSEM were not read. Also: SBG's chain is unkeyed and forgeable, verify does not re-execute, and observations are taken as recorded, so "independently checkable" is limited to logical consistency. | C2 explicit sufficiency (recorded change-exercise measure conditioning a no-divergence verdict) | Partially novel, only as a gate | **Not novel** for the measure; **Partially novel** for the verdict gate only | Evans & Savoia state the ambiguity; Cleverest ranks reached-but-unchanged below output change; DiffTestGen and Testora measure change coverage; patch-coverage studies exist. | C3 evidence-grounded insufficient outcome (three-way, never equivalence) | Partially novel (narrow) | **Partially novel (narrow)**; the three-way idea is **Not novel** | DiffKemp (full text) and Prefactory, ORION, Cleverest (tool-mediated) have non-binary outcomes; Evans & Savoia name the ambiguity; reject option is old. Net: the defensible statement is a design-level integration (C1 schema + C3 grounds + C2 gate + C4 typing) whose parts each have prior art and whose benefit is unmeasured.

## 5. Statements the manuscript must NOT make

1. That SBG, any of C1 to C4 or the combination is "the first", "novel" (unqualified) or "new" relative to Evans & Savoia, Shadow, Cleverest, Prefactory, DiffKemp, Testora, DiffTestGen, PatchGuru, ORION, SemaDiff or Mokav.
2. That prior work lacks witnesses, change coverage, an inconclusive outcome or flakiness handling (all four exist in the accessed texts); and that any tool "does not" do X when only a tool-mediated, abstract or metadata source was read.
3. That a no-divergence verdict means equivalence, correctness, safety, or absence of regression. `NO_DIVERGENCE_OBSERVED` is relative to the supplied inputs, channels, harness H and recorded activation.
4. Any detection, false-assurance, precision/recall, abstention-quality, or superiority claim for SBG relative to any named tool or to simple baselines; any claim that S1/S2 levels discriminate correct from incorrect verdicts (thresholds UNVALIDATED; red team found no discrimination).
5. That E1 shows determinism, a determinism rate improvement, or that the hash-seed fix reduced quarantine (SUPPORTED weakly only as "did not rise", 1 vs 2 pairs, confounded). That hash-order dependence is covered (it is untested) or that `datetime.now` programs are handled.
6. That K1 or K2 is met, cleared or triggered; that results are independent, confirmatory, pre-registered as a whole, or on pristine data.
7. That evidence packages are tamper-evident, tamper-proof, immutable or independently validated (plaintext unkeyed chain, forgeable by any writer, no trust anchor, `verify` does not re-execute), or that execution is OS-isolated or validated (macOS `sandbox-exec` only, `policy_below_minimum=true`, Linux Phase 3).
8. That SBG generates or discovers divergent inputs, handles real repository PRs, multi-file changes, classes/generators, renames or non-Python code (not implemented).
9. That the 2026 preprints' numbers (Testora, DiffTestGen, PatchGuru, ORION, SemaDiff, Prefactory) were reproduced or are comparable with each other; they are the authors' own and were processed from a summarising tool.
10. That the INSUFFICIENT rate is a measure of anything beyond engine gaps and conservative blocking on development data, or that the 37-pair NDO/S2 to `unmodelled_change` shift reduced false assurance (only 3 pairs inspected).
