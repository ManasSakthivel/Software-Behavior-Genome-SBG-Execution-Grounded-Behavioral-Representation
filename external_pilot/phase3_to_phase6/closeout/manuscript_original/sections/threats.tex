Two of these threats deserve emphasis. First, the corpus was not held out: the integrity-first ordering of the trust gate and the relevant-evidence profile fix were both responses to \ers{} results on the same records. This favours \sbg{}, yet its predictive comparison remained negative. Second, every number in this paper was recomputed or counted from machine-readable records. Values that existed only in report text are listed as excluded in the replication package.
