# Evidence-Calibrated No-Divergence Verdicts for Differential Behavioural Regression Analysis: A Pilot Study and a Negative Result on Evidence-Response Features

**Status: draft manuscript (markdown), framing F1 with F2/F3/F4 (negative result, pilot data, influence only a proxy, trust not validated). Not compiled to IEEE format; no readiness claim. All empirical results are development-data, exploratory.**

## Abstract
A differential check that observes no difference between two program versions is easily read as reassurance although it only reports what the chosen inputs showed. We define the *false-assurance risk* R = P(F=1 | NO_DIVERGENCE_OBSERVED issued) relative to a declared observation boundary and adjudication budget (not equivalence), implement a change-conditioned evidence profile, an Evidence Response Signature (ERS) that records how the estimated risk and decision change under nine pre-declared controlled evidence perturbations, and a transparent monotone calibrator with nine baselines and feature ablations. On 312 development pairs (258 labelled, 101 projects, design-exposed, non-independent) the full evidence profile with ERS did not demonstrably improve false-assurance risk at matched coverage over coverage-only calibration (difference +1.6 percentage points, 95% project-cluster CI [-6.5, +16.2]); held-out log loss was lower by about 0.03 with ERS in three seeds, but every interval included zero. No calibrated model beat the base-rate Brier score by more than noise. The study is underpowered (61 issued verdicts at the reference coverage; minimum detectable effect about 16 points). We report the ERS control matrix (all nine perturbations behaved as pre-declared after one disclosed policy fix), an implemented but unexecuted blinded-adjudication workflow, and a forgery suite in which 2 of 15 attacks still succeed. We make no claim of generalisation, causal effect, equivalence, or security isolation.

## 1 Introduction and problem
Empty difference sets are ambiguous (Evans and Savoia 2007). The V7-B engine of this project issues NO_DIVERGENCE_OBSERVED under a fixed sufficiency gate; Phase 2 showed its lower false assurance than plain differential testing was entirely abstention and that its no-difference verdicts were not more reliable (13/61 wrong versus 24/130). V8 asks whether estimated risk can be made informative from the evidence itself.
## 2 Related work (positioning only; full review in `research/v8_final_design/01_PRIOR_ART_REVIEW.md`)
Evidence deletion to test an adequacy measure is prior art (Schuler and Zeller 2011); leave-out evidence as adequacy is BESTEST (Fraser and Walkinshaw 2015); behavioural gaps in documented behaviour are measured by Paul and Holmes (2026, preprint); selective risk and conformal risk control (Angelopoulos et al. 2024) supply the risk-control vocabulary and its assumptions; differential-testing tools (Shadow, Testora, Cleverest and others) expose differences rather than assessing empty results. We claim no novelty for any of these; the contribution is the estimand, the ERS protocol and the matched-coverage evaluation.
## 3 Estimand
R = P(Y=1 | A=1) with A the event that NDO is issued and Y the adjudicated outcome; the estimate is relative to the observation boundary and budget and lower-bounds true risk when adjudication is incomplete. Unresolved cases are never counted as non-divergent: three variants (dropped, worst case, optimistic) are provided.
## 4 Method
Evidence profile (15 features; activation, branch coverage, multiplicity, repetition, a proxy for observable influence, effective diversity, reproducibility, loss, channel completeness, provenance, integrity, environment). ERS: for perturbation j, Delta_j = R_hat(E_j') - R_hat(E), stored with original and perturbed profile, risk, decision, coverage, provenance and integrity status, expected and observed response and pass flag; a descriptive change in estimated risk, not causal. Calibrator: monotone-sign L2-penalised logistic regression fitted on eligible pairs, thresholds chosen on inner project folds. Nine baselines and ablations (coverage plus repetition, multiplicity, influence proxy, provenance and integrity; full profile without ERS).
## 5 Perturbation protocol (P1-P9)
Relevant deletion, irrelevant and duplicate augmentation, observation masking, reproducibility-budget perturbation, independent discriminating evidence, provenance degradation, integrity violation, environment inconsistency. On the 312 development records with the fitted model: P1 16/0/296 (pass/fail/not applicable), P2 227/0/85, P3 262/0/50, P4 178/0/134, P5 176/0/136, P6 228/0/84, P7 178/0/134, P8 312/0/0, P9 228/0/84. A first run failed P8 on 84 witnessed pairs (integrity violation did not downgrade a divergence); a V8 trust gate rule resolved it and the first run is preserved. P2, P5 and P6 were run post hoc on recorded evidence only; real-execution variants were not run.
## 6 Trusted evidence collection
V8 captures per-input hit and branch sets parent-side without modifying V7-B, adds a shadow re-observation, and runs a 15-attack forgery suite: 9 detected, 3 blocked by the engine, 1 without effect, 2 still succeed (hiding a stdout divergence; editing unverified fields with sidecar rebinding). The harness shares the program's address space; isolation is macOS-only; the hash chain is tamper-evident, not proof of truth. No security property is claimed.
## 7 Experimental design
Project-grouped nested cross-validation, project-cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples), matched-coverage comparison with within-fold rank matching, Holm correction, Brier, log loss, calibration slope and intercept, reliability bins, risk-coverage curves. Pre-declared primary hypothesis: at matched coverage CEC reduces risk versus coverage-only calibration by at least 5 points with positive lower bound.
## 8 Dataset and adjudication
Development only: 321 pairs (312 driven; 258 labelled; 54 unresolved or suspect labels excluded and counted). Labels are generator or construction labels. The blinded two-adjudicator workflow (frozen boundary and budget, no model outputs in the case schema, Cohen's kappa, audit trail) is implemented and tested on synthetic fixtures; no real adjudication has occurred. Independent evaluation and the holdout are pending and gated.
## 9 Results (development data; seeds 0-2)
Rule baselines (coverage, risk): B1 0.709, 0.169; V7-B gate 0.236, 0.213; coverage-only gate 0.547, 0.121. Calibrated models (eligible n=85, base-rate Brier 0.203): coverage-only calibration Brier 0.225 and log loss 0.649; CEC without ERS 0.221 and 0.645; full CEC 0.207 and 0.615; multiplicity-only 0.212 and 0.620; calibration slopes -1.83, -0.02, 0.30, 0.21. Primary comparison at the V7-B gate coverage: CEC risk 0.213 versus 0.230 for coverage-only calibration (+0.016, 95% CI [-0.065, 0.162]); versus the V7-B gate 0.000; versus random abstention -0.044. The 5-point hypothesis was not met in any seed. Honest thresholds issued almost nothing at risk targets 0.10 and 0.15.
## 10 Ablation and confound analysis
Coverage adds nothing measurable beyond the V7-B activation screen; multiplicity is the only consistent signal (negative coefficient in every first-fold fit); ERS lowers held-out log loss by about 0.03 in each seed with intervals including zero and may restate multiplicity (P1 applies to 16 of 312 records). Conclusion: ERS has no demonstrated incremental value over coverage multiplicity on these data; absence of evidence at this power is not evidence of absence.
## 11 Distribution shift
Not tested: the development data lack family diversity (one project dominates; synthetic and BugsInPy do not give independent families). Deferred, not simulated.
## 12 Threats to validity
See `V8_THREATS_TO_VALIDITY.md`: design-exposed data, generator labels, low power, eligibility-rule choice, redundancy of ERS with multiplicity, proxy influence, post-hoc perturbations, trust limits, no real adjudication.
## 13 Reproducibility
Commands, seeds and commits are in `V8_REPRODUCIBILITY_GUIDE.md`; compact result files in `results/v8_dev_summary*`; raw evidence archived on the working machine.
## 14 Conclusion
On development data the evidence-response information gave no demonstrated gain over coverage and multiplicity for estimating false-assurance risk at matched coverage, and the data are too small to settle the question. The implemented pipeline, the matched-coverage protocol and the ERS controls are ready for an independent, adjudicated, project-split evaluation, which has not been run and requires authorisation.
